HEALTH DATA PRIVACY NOTICE

Sovereign Matrix takes the confidentiality of your health data extremely seriously. Before enabling Apple Watch Integration, please read and agree to the following terms:

1. Scope of Data Read: If enabled, this application will request read access to your Apple HealthKit database for heart rate (resting/exercise), daily steps, active energy burned, and standing hours.

2. Purpose of Use: This telemetry is exclusively used by the local OntologyMedicalEngine SAA solver to perform cardiovascular and metabolic risk cross-referencing against your static lab records.

3. Encryption & Sandboxing: All HealthKit data is processed strictly within sandboxed memory. We enforce zero persistent tracking on external servers. No telemetry is shared with third parties.

4. Consent Revocation: You can revoke permission at any time in your iOS Settings -> Health app or by toggling this switch off.
